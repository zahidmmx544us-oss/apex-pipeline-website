Our 7-Stage Data Verification Protocol

Cheap brokers sell outdated, recycled CSVs with 20% bounce rates that destroy your domains. Apex Pipeline Media runs every single record through a proprietary 7-stage validation pipeline before it touches your campaign.

STAGE 01

Syntax & Regex Filter

Instant removal of malformed email syntax, duplicate entries, rogue characters, and RFC-noncompliant addresses.

STAGE 02

MX & DNS Record Ping

Real-time server handshake confirming that the domain has functional, receiving mail exchange (MX) servers.

STAGE 03

SMTP Handshake Verification

Live inbox simulation directly pinging the recipient's mail server without sending an actual message to verify address existence.

STAGE 04

Spam Trap & Honeypot Scrub

Scrubbing against known blacklists, spam trap networks, disposable temporary mail providers, and role-based accounts (info@, support@).

STAGE 05

Catch-All Server Analysis

Advanced diagnostic checks on accept-all mail servers using proprietary pattern recognition and bounce prediction scoring.

STAGE 06

LinkedIn Employment Verification

Cross-verification that the decision-maker is still actively employed in the stated executive role as of the past 14 days.

Stage 07: The 98%+ Delivery Guarantee

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Apex Pipeline Media.

How is Apex Pipeline Media different from data brokers like ZoomInfo or Apollo?

Database brokers sell massive, self-serve directories that contain outdated emails, retired executives, and spam traps. You still have to buy expensive tools, set up secondary domains, write copy, and manage responses. We are an end-to-end partner: we extract fresh, real-time data, verify it through our 7-stage protocol, manage outbound infrastructure, and deliver booked meetings straight onto your calendar.

Will cold email outreach damage our primary domain reputation?

Never. We never send outbound outreach from your core company domain. We purchase and configure isolated secondary domains (e.g. getapexmedia.com or tryapex.com) that redirect to your main site. We configure complete SPF, DKIM, and DMARC protocols and warm up each mailbox for 21 days before sending any campaign.

What happens if an email bounces?

We provide a strict < 2% bounce rate guarantee. If any record supplied in your campaigns bounces beyond this threshold, we credit and replace those records 2-for-1 immediately.

How fast can we launch campaigns?

Our standard onboarding takes 14 days. This window is strictly required to properly register secondary domains, configure authentication records, and perform essential automated mailbox warmup to guarantee maximum deliverability.

How do you qualify booked appointments?

Before any prospect is booked onto your calendar, our inbox managers verify that they match your specific revenue tier, employee count, and purchasing authority. We never book low-level gatekeepers or unqualified tire-kickers.
